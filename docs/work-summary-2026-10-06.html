Work summary

Kharka Farm OS · Livestock monorepo · 6 Oct 2026
Live at https://evoqedlivestockfarm.vercel.app · Commit f5a5bcd, pushed to main

In one paragraph. Today the farm app gained four things. Each person can now save a Nepali mobile number and receive urgent reminders by SMS through Sparrow SMS. Reminders now go to the right people by role: crucial ones reach the admin and everyone else, and day-to-day field jobs go only to workers. On mobile, tapping the round profile icon shows the person's account details instead of logging them out. And the admin has a member management screen (a small CMS) to edit, deactivate, reset passwords for, and remove the people on their farm. We also connected the live Vercel address everywhere it was needed, fixed several Expo Go problems on the emulator, and produced the notification-reminders PDF.

  • Production checked: the website and API at evoqedlivestockfarm.vercel.app respond and are healthy.

2. SMS alerts with a phone number

Phone number field

  • Web: an "SMS alerts" card at the top of the Inbox where each person saves their own number.
  • Mobile: the same card in the Inbox and inside the new profile sheet.
  • Admin: a phone field when adding a member and when editing one.
  • Validation: only Nepali mobiles are accepted: 10 digits starting with 98, 97 or 96. People can type 98XXXXXXXX, +977… or with spaces; it is stored in the international form +97798XXXXXXXX.

Sparrow SMS

Sparrow SMS was chosen as the best Nepali provider: it is cheaper and more reliable for NTC and Ncell numbers than an international service. The API picks a provider automatically:

When these are setWhat happens
SPARROW_SMS_TOKEN and SPARROW_SMS_FROMSMS is sent through Sparrow SMS.
Twilio settings onlySMS is sent through Twilio (kept as a backup option).
NeitherNothing is sent; the message is only written to the server log. Push notifications still work.

SMS is used only for the most urgent reminders, as described in the notification-reminders PDF.

3. Who gets which reminder

Before today every member received every reminder. Now each reminder is sent according to its priority and type:

ReminderWho receives it
Critical (any type)Everyone on the farm: admin, manager, workers, and the vet if it is a health task.
Assigned to a personOnly that person. They always receive their own task.
Management (milk yield drop, stock reorder and expiry, tank variance, breeding decisions, expense approval, unpaid sales)Admin and manager.
Field work (everything else: colostrum, heat checks, feeding, calving follow-ups and similar)Workers only. If the farm has no workers it goes to the manager, and if there is no manager, to the admin.
Health tasks (urgent vet call, treatment follow-up, pregnancy check, calf health check, no-heat vet visit)The vet, but only when the task is critical or nobody is assigned.
Cooperative accountsNever receive farm reminders.
Deactivated peopleNever receive reminders.

4. Mobile profile sheet

Tapping the round initial icon in the top bar used to log the person out immediately. It now opens a sheet with their name, email, role, farm and farm mode, connection status, and their SMS phone number. Sign out is a separate red button inside the sheet, so nobody is logged out by accident.

5. Admin member management (CMS)

On web this is Admin → Members; on mobile, the Admin screen. The admin can:

  • See everyone with their name, email, phone, role, active status and last sign-in, plus a count of total and active people.
  • Search and filter by name, email or phone, by role, and by active or inactive.
  • Edit a person: name, email, phone, role, whether they can sign in, and voice-call alerts.
  • Reset a password: sets a new temporary password and signs the person out on every phone and computer.
  • Remove from the farm: after a confirmation. Their past records stay; tasks assigned to them become unassigned. If this was their only farm, their account is also deactivated.
  • Add members with name, email, phone, role, temporary password and voice alerts.

Safety rules enforced by the server

RuleWhat it prevents
No self-lockoutAn admin cannot change their own role, deactivate themselves or remove themselves. Their row is marked "You".
Always one adminThe farm must keep at least one active admin.
Shared accountsIf a person also belongs to another farm, only they can change their email or password.
Unique emailTwo people cannot share one email address.
Sessions revokedA password reset or deactivation signs the person out everywhere straight away.
Audit logEvery add, edit, password reset and removal is recorded in Admin → Audit.

6. Expo Go fixes on the emulator

  • "Unable to resolve expo-notifications" and an old API address: Metro was stuck waiting at an "Install recommended Expo Go version?" prompt and kept serving an old bundle. Restarting it fixed this.
  • "Something went wrong" in Expo Go: it was opening a saved project at an old Wi-Fi address. The PC's address changes, so the app is now opened at exp://10.0.2.2:8081, the emulator's fixed address for the PC.
