Notifications & Reminders

Kharka Farm OS · Livestock monorepo · Prepared 3 Oct 2026
Covers the API (apps/api), mobile app (apps/mobile) and web Inbox (apps/web).

In one paragraph. Every reminder is a task in the farm's Inbox. Tasks are created automatically from farm events (a calving, a heat, a service, low stock, an unpaid sale) or by scheduled checks. A dispatcher then delivers each due task as a push notification, and for the most urgent ones also by SMS and a recorded Nepali voice call. Anti-fatigue rules hold back non-urgent alerts at night, cap how many a farmer gets per day, and bundle similar ones together. Urgent alerts that nobody acknowledges escalate to the manager, then the owner. The phone also keeps alarms for urgent tasks so they still ring with no signal.

1. How a reminder reaches the farmer

  1. A task is created. Triggered by an event (e.g. recording a calving creates the colostrum reminders), or by the nightly / hourly checks (e.g. stock below reorder level). Creating the same task twice changes nothing.
  2. It appears in the Inbox on web and mobile, with a button that opens the right form.
  3. The dispatcher sends it when it falls due, applying the delivery rules in section 2.
  4. The phone schedules an offline alarm for urgent task types, so it fires even without internet.
  5. The farmer acts — Do, Done, Snooze or Dismiss — and the task closes.

Delivery channels

ChannelUsed forHow it works
Push notification Every reminder Sent through Firebase Cloud Messaging (installed app) or the Expo push service (Expo Go testing). The phone registers its push token after login. Android channels: Critical farm alerts (max importance) and Farm reminders.
SMS Critical alerts, and High alerts overdue by 24 h Sent through Sparrow SMS (Nepal, NTC + Ncell), with Twilio as a fallback. Goes to the mobile number each person saves in the Inbox SMS alerts card, or that an admin sets on the Members page (Nepal mobiles only, e.g. 98XXXXXXXX). Capped at 40 SMS per farm per Nepali month (SMS_MONTHLY_CAP). Nepali text uses 70 characters per SMS segment, English 160.
Voice call Critical alerts and escalations Built from pre-recorded Nepali voice clips spliced together (e.g. "colostrum" + "number is" + digits). Never machine text-to-speech. Queued for now; not dialled yet.
Offline phone alarm Urgent types (section 5) Scheduled on the phone itself from tasks synced in the last 30 days, so it rings with no signal.
Inbox Everything Web and mobile list of open tasks with actions. Works offline from the last synced copy.

2. Delivery rules (anti-fatigue)

RuleWhat it does
Quiet hours 20:00 – 05:00 Non-critical alerts are held and delivered at 05:00 Nepal time. CRITICAL alerts always go through immediately.
Buffalo silent-heat exception The buffalo silent-heat check is allowed out at 04:00, because buffalo show heat mostly before dawn. On by default for buffalo farms; can be switched off per user.
Daily cap At most 5 non-critical pushes per person per day. Extra ones stay in the Inbox but don't buzz the phone.
Grouping Same-type alerts are bundled into one push, e.g. "6 animals to check for heat this morning" or "4 animals due for FMD". Never grouped: colostrum, calving watch, urgent vet, and sync-protocol injections — one animal, one alert.
SMS gate SMS only for Critical alerts, or High alerts more than 24 h overdue, and only while the monthly cap has room.
Escalation An unacknowledged Critical alert escalates to the manager after 30 minutes and the owner after 60 minutes.
Mute offer After a user dismisses the same reminder type 3 times, they're offered to mute it for 14 days. Never offered for Critical types.
Expiry Open tasks more than 30 days past due are marked expired by the nightly job.

When things run

JobSchedule (Nepal time)Purpose
DispatcherEvery 5 minutes Sends due tasks as push / SMS / voice, applying the rules above.
Hourly jobEvery hour, on the hour Service windows closing / missed; escalates unacknowledged Critical alerts.
Nightly job01:00 daily Breeding stages and stage reminders, anestrus ladder, protocol checks, vaccinations, withhold ends, stock, missing milk, finance, profit metrics, and expiring stale tasks.
On Vercel: serverless hosting doesn't run timers. The dispatcher is triggered every 5 minutes by the GitHub Action notification-dispatch.yml calling POST /v1/notifications/dispatch. The hourly and nightly jobs do not yet have an external trigger on Vercel, so in production their reminders are only created by in-app events until one is added.

4. Inbox actions

  • Do — opens the right form directly (e.g. colostrum form, pregnancy check, Expenses).
  • Done — closes the task. Works offline; it syncs when back online.
  • Snooze — +1 hour, +1 day or +1 week.
  • Dismiss — with a reason: not needed, already done offline, animal sold, wrong animal, other. Works offline.
  • Active / Completed tabs and a Mine only filter.
  • Marker tasks can only be closed by scanning the animal's tag.
  • Mute offer appears after 3 dismissals of the same type.
  • Titles show in the user's chosen language (English or Nepali).

5. Offline phone alarms

The app schedules alarms on the phone for these types, using tasks synced in the last 30 days. They ring at the due time even with no internet:

Colostrum · Calving watch · Urgent vet · Vaccination due · Medication dose · Service window · Low stock · Expense approval · Unpaid revenue

Heat-watch reminders are deliberately not offline alarms because they come every morning for many animals; they arrive as push notifications when online.
