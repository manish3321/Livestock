Work summary

Kharka Farm OS · Livestock monorepo · 15 Oct 2026
Live at https://evoqedlivestockfarm.vercel.app · Three commits pushed to main, from 70260e5 to c716c9d · Three Android builds

In one paragraph. Today fixed three things people noticed while using the app. Syncing is faster: "Syncing…" now clears in about half a second instead of waiting several seconds for every screen to re-download, and the dashboard asks the database for everything at once. In the Shed, picking Vaccination, Treatment, Evening or Midday now opens what you picked instead of morning milking, and "waiting to sync: 2" no longer stays on screen while online. Finally, mistakes can now be corrected: a worker can edit or delete a record, but the change is only applied after an Admin or Manager approves it, on the phone or the website.

1. Faster syncing

Why it was slow

  • Too much in each sync. "Syncing…" stayed on while the app sent your changes, fetched new ones, and then re-downloaded the saved copies of all 18 screens (dashboard, animals, expenses, reports and so on). Sending and fetching changes takes about half a second. The 18 screens took about 3 seconds from a PC, and longer on mobile data.
  • Slow dashboard. Its six-month money chart asked the database for one month at a time, so it waited on six round trips in a row. On its own the dashboard took about 3 seconds.
  • Repeated downloads. Coming back online ran the full download twice, and several screens could start a sync at the same moment.

What changed

  • App: "Syncing…" only covers sending and fetching changes. The saved screen copies refresh quietly in the background, at most every 5 minutes, or straight away after you record something. Overlapping syncs share one run. Login and app start no longer wait for the screen copies.
  • API: the dashboard sends all its database queries at once, and the six-month chart uses 2 queries instead of 6 rounds of 2. Every number was checked against the live site and came out the same.

2. Shed fixes

Every option opened morning milking

  • Cause: while a morning milking round was open, the Shed screen kept copying that round's job and session back over your choice, every few seconds during sync. The only button was "Resume round", which always reopened the morning milking round.
  • Fix: the open round's job and session are shown only when you first arrive; your taps are never overridden. "Resume round" appears only when your choice matches the open round. Otherwise the button is "Start this session" and opens what you picked. Starting a job that is already open just reopens it, so nothing is duplicated.

"Waiting to sync: 2" while online

  • Cause: each shed save queues two items (the record and a scan log), but saving never started a sync, so they waited until the app went offline and back, or until another screen synced.
  • Fix: every shed save sends right away, and finishing a round sends its entries first. If the internet drops mid-send, the items stay queued and retry later; before, they were marked failed and never retried. The new app also retries items that older versions gave up on.

3. Correcting mistakes with approval

Anyone who can record data can now also correct it. Changes by workers wait for approval; Admins and Managers can approve them, and their own edits apply straight away (still recorded in the audit log).

1. Worker editsChanges a record or deletes it, optionally with a reason.
→
2. Checked and heldInvalid data is refused at once. Valid changes are saved as a request and the record stays as it was. The worker sees "Sent for approval".
→
3. Admin or Manager reviewsApprovals shows what the value is now and what it will become, with a note box.
→
4. Approve or rejectApprove applies the change exactly as if the worker had been allowed; Reject leaves the record untouched.

What it covers

  • Records: animal details and weights, health records, heat and breeding records, inventory, groups, fish ponds, batches, revenue and milk entries.
  • Milk in the shed: fixing a milk entry while its round is still open, or within 12 hours, applies straight away, so the shed's "change if needed" step keeps working.
  • Permissions don't change: a worker who isn't allowed to delete animals still can't, even with approval.
  • Corrections need the internet. A worker's edit saved offline on the phone is refused when it syncs, so corrections should be made while online. New records still save offline and sync as before.

Where to find it

  • Website: a new Approvals link in the menu, with a red count of waiting requests for Admins and Managers. Tabs for Waiting and Done. Workers see their own requests there and can withdraw one that is still waiting.
  • Phone: an Approvals button in the Inbox with the waiting count, opening the same list. After an edit the worker sees a "Sent for approval" message.
  • Both in English and Nepali.

Tested against the API

An end-to-end run of 14 checks passed: invalid edits refused with nothing queued; a worker edit held (202) and listed with the old value; workers can't approve (403); an Admin approval applies the change and a second approval is refused (409); a rejection leaves the animal unchanged; workers can withdraw; Admin edits apply directly; other worker screens are unaffected. The test requests were cleaned up afterwards.
